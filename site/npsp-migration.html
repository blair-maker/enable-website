Moving from the Nonprofit Success Pack to Nonprofit Cloud.

NPSP remains supported and Salesforce has announced no forced end date. That makes this a decision rather than a deadline, which is the best possible time to make it carefully. Start with an assessment, not a migration plan.

Where we stand on this

Enable has years of Salesforce nonprofit experience and delivers Nonprofit Cloud today. NPSP migration is an offering we are bringing to market now, rather than a decade-long track record we are going to claim. What that means in practice: the assessment is priced small and sold on its own, the findings are yours whether or not you continue, and we will tell you plainly where the ground is new for everyone.

The first question is whether to move at all

Plenty of organisations are running NPSP well and should keep doing so for now. The cost of moving is real, and a migration undertaken because of a rumour about end-of-life is a migration done for the wrong reason.

Reasons to move now

You need programme, case or outcome management and are currently bolting it on. You are buying new Salesforce capability that assumes core objects. Your customisation of NPSP has become the thing that blocks every upgrade.

Reasons to wait

NPSP is doing what you need, your customisation is light, and the money would do more good spent on the mission this year. Waiting is a legitimate answer and we will give it where it is the right one.

Reasons it is not the real problem

If reporting is wrong, adoption is poor or data quality is bad, those follow you across. A migration is an expensive way to discover that the problem was the process. We would rather find that first.

What actually changes

Not a rename. The data model underneath is different, which is why a migration is a rebuild of your fundraising operation rather than a data load.

A managed package sitting on Sales Cloud Core platform objects, no package to upgrade
Household Accounts and Contacts Person Accounts with household modelling
Donations recorded as Opportunities Gift Transactions, with Gift Commitments for recurring giving
General Accounting Units and Allocations Gift Designations and default designations
Customizable Rollups Data Processing Engine and the Business Rules Engine
Affiliations and Relationships Party relationships on the core model
Engagement Plans Action Plans
Programme delivery in custom objects, usually Program Management, Case Management and Outcome Management

The assessment

Three weeks, fixed price

Priced small and deliberately separate, so you can stop after it without having bought a migration.

What we look at

Your NPSP customisation, data volumes and quality, integrations, reporting, recurring giving setup, and what your team actually uses day to day.

What you get

A written recommendation with a real number: move, wait, or fix something else first. Plus a sequenced plan if the answer is move, and the findings to act on yourself if it is not.

If the answer is move

01

Model first, migrate second

Constituents, households, designations and recurring giving modelled in a sandbox and tested against your real data before anything is committed. The model is the migration.

02

Run in parallel where it is possible

Financial year boundaries, appeal cycles and audit periods decide the cutover, not the project plan. Where a parallel period is feasible we use it.

03

History, receipting and the audit trail

Gift history, acknowledgements and the numbers your auditor and your funders rely on, reconciled before cutover rather than explained afterwards.
