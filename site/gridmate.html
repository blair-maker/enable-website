Your team keeps exporting to a spreadsheet. This is why.

Salesforce makes editing one record easy and editing forty records painful. Enable implements GridMate, a suite of 32 Lightning components that closes the gap: real inline editing, grids, calendars, kanban and maps, on the records you already have.

Thirty-two components, aimed squarely at the reasons people leave Salesforce.

Every export to Excel is a small vote against the platform, and a data quality problem waiting to happen. GridMate removes the reason for the export rather than writing a policy against it.

Grids and inline editing

User Grid, Pivot Grid, List View Grid and Related List Grid. Update many fields and record types at once, with four levels of filtering, grouping and sorting.

Calendars and Gantt

Multi, single and compact calendars plus a Gantt chart, for teams scheduling work against records rather than in a separate tool.

Kanban and maps

Kanban boards on list views and field sets, map lists and record maps, for work that is easier to see as a board or a territory than as a table.

Layout and navigation

Record cards, object timelines, dynamic tabs and accordions, and a file explorer. Related records visible and editable without switching tab.

RevenueMate, from the same team

Salesforce CPQ has entered end-of-sale, and mid-market organisations now need a path that is not a Revenue Cloud programme. RevenueMate is a Salesforce-native CPQ built by the GridMate team, aimed at exactly that gap: enterprise capability without the implementation that usually comes with it.

Components are cheap. Deciding where they belong is the work.

Dropping 32 components onto every page makes an org worse, not better. We work out which views actually cause the spreadsheet exports, and fix those.

Find the actual friction

We watch how your team really works and find the three or four screens where people give up and export. Those are the ones worth rebuilding.

Configure to the role

A grid for a service team and a grid for a finance team are different grids. Filters, columns, permissions and record-type behaviour set per role.

Protect the data model

Bulk editing is powerful in both directions. Validation, field-level security and sharing checked before anyone can change forty records at once.

Measure whether it worked

Adoption before and after, and report exports tracked. If the spreadsheet habit does not fall, the change did not work and we say so.
