The organisation

Farm Focus makes farm financial management software for New Zealand and Australian farmers and growers: cashflow planning, GST returns, livestock reconciliation and reporting, in one place. It began in 1981 when three Wairarapa farmers wanted a better way to forecast a farm business, and the product that came out of it, Cash Manager, ran for forty years before the company rebranded to Farm Focus in 2021.

It is based in Masterton and serves thousands of customers across sheep, beef and dairy, from single operators through to family trusts and corporate farms. Its recent move to a cloud platform is the backdrop most technology conversations there now sit against.
