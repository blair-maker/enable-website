Most commerce projects finish with two systems and an expensive sync between them.

The storefront goes on Shopify or WooCommerce. The customer record stays in Salesforce. Between them sits a connector, a nightly job and someone whose real job has quietly become reconciling orders. It holds together until it doesn't, and the first sign is usually a report that no longer matches the one beside it.

Two customer records

Someone who buys online and again at the counter becomes two contacts, and neither history is complete.

Reports that disagree

Finance, marketing and service each quote a different number, because each is reading a different system.

Integration as a running cost

Middleware licences, connector upgrades and the one developer who understands them become a permanent line item.

Automation that stops at the border

Salesforce cannot act on an abandoned cart, a stock hold or a warranty claim it never sees.

StoreConnect is an AppExchange package. The store runs inside Salesforce.

There is no API layer and no external database. Products, pricing, stock, orders, customers, pages and content are Salesforce records. Your admins already know how to change them, your reports already know where to find them, and your Flows can act on them the moment they are created.

Website and CMS

Pages, blogs, navigation and media managed as Salesforce records, with full control of the theme, CSS and custom JavaScript. Media is served through a CDN.

eCommerce

Physical goods, digital products, events, bookings, subscriptions, memberships and donations. Promotions, loyalty points, gift cards and account credit, with no order limits.

Point of sale

Card terminals, cash drawers, receipt and label printers, scanners and roving tablets, with no hardware lock-in and an offline mode that resyncs when the connection returns.

Multi-store, multi-currency

Several storefronts, brands or regions on one back end, with their own pricebooks, region-based tax and shared stock. Sell in NZD and anything else at the same time.

Agentforce agents

Commerce agents grounded in your own catalogue, policies and order history. They read the same records your staff do, so there is no knowledge base to retrain when a price changes.

Security and compliance

SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS 4.0.1 and GDPR, on Salesforce infrastructure. One system to certify instead of three, and one less place cardholder data can sit.

Before you ask

Do we need Experience Cloud licences?

No. The customer account portal does not require them, and it works with SSO if you already run a portal of your own.

What happens to our ERP or accounting system?

It stays where it is. StoreConnect sits in Salesforce alongside the integrations you already have, and the StoreConnect ecosystem covers Xero, GoldFinch, NAO and others natively.

Can we keep our current website design?

Yes. The theme is fully adjustable, with custom CSS and JavaScript, so we rebuild the front end to your existing brand rather than asking you to accept a template.

How long does an implementation take?

A single storefront on a healthy Salesforce org is a matter of weeks. Multi-store, multi-currency or point of sale rollouts take longer. The fit assessment is where you get a real date.

Does it work properly in New Zealand?

Yes. Multi-currency and region-based tax are standard, several payment and shipping providers are supported, and local region hosting is available on the higher plans.
