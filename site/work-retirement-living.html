The work / Retirement living

Every enquiry was reaching the same person.

A retirement living operator was generating strong enquiry volume across several villages. Almost all of it was landing in one consultant’s queue, because of a routing fallback nobody had revisited since go-live.

Talk to us about similar work More of the work
What the routing was doing

The rules read correctly. A fallback written as a safety net had become the main path.

The situation

Marketing was working. Enquiries were arriving. But follow-up was slow and uneven, and leadership could not see why. The routing rules looked correct on paper. What had happened was more ordinary: a fallback assignment, written as a safety net at go-live, had quietly become the main path, because several village-level users had been deactivated over the years and duplicates created in their place. Every enquiry that failed to match an active user fell through to the same queue and the same person.

What we built

01

Traced the routing, not the symptom

The reported problem was slow follow-up. The actual problem was assignment. We followed real enquiries through the rules rather than reading the configuration and assuming it did what it said.

02

Rebuilt assignment around the village

Routing keyed to the village the person is enquiring about, with an owner per village and a fallback that alerts somebody rather than silently absorbing the work.

03

Cleaned up the user estate

Inactive users and their duplicates identified and resolved, which is unglamorous and was the actual root cause.

04

Made tours visible on the account

Tour bookings were being recorded against a child object that the account page never showed, so a booked visit was invisible to anyone looking at the person. We surfaced it, and fixed a re-entrant save loop found along the way.

What changed

Enquiries reach the right village

Follow-up is now done by the person who knows the site the enquirer is asking about, which changes the quality of the conversation as much as the speed.

One consultant got their week back

The person absorbing the fallback was carrying work that was never meant to be theirs, and no report showed it.

Leadership can see the funnel

Enquiry through to tour through to outcome, per village, from records rather than from a manually maintained spreadsheet.

What we would tell you

Nobody had done anything wrong here. A sensible safety net at go-live became the default path over several years of ordinary staff turnover, and no report was looking at assignment. It is worth someone checking routing fallbacks annually, which is exactly the kind of thing a quarterly platform review catches.
