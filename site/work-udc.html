What these three products do together.

This is what the combination is for in general, not a claim about what was delivered at UDC. Read it as context for the products listed above.

Data 360 underneath

A finance customer exists across origination, servicing, collections and whatever the broker channel knows. Data 360 is where those become one person, with the survivorship rules written down rather than assumed.

Marketing Cloud Next on top

Journeys, consent and campaigns built on that resolved customer rather than on a list export. Consent in particular is a compliance artefact in financial services, not a marketing preference.

Salesforce Intelligence to measure it

The part most marketing stacks skip: attribution and performance measured against the same customer definition the campaigns were built on, so the numbers and the audience cannot quietly disagree.

How we write these up.

1

The client reads it first

Every case study on this site is sent to the client before it is published. If they will not put their name to the wording, the wording changes or the page stays a draft.

2

No numbers we cannot show our working for

Percentage uplifts are easy to write and hard to defend. If a figure is not measured, it does not appear.

3

What went wrong stays in

Engagements have bad weeks. Removing them makes every case study read the same and tells a prospective client nothing.
